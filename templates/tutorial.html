📚 How It Works

Learn to backtest trading strategies on the Nepal Stock Exchange.

1

Pick a NEPSE Stock

Choose from 50+ actively traded stocks listed on NEPSE — commercial banks, hydropower, insurance, microfinance, and more. You can also search by name or filter by sector.

2

Set Your Date Range

Select any period from 2020 to July 2026. The longer the range, the more reliable your backtest results. Data covers pre-COVID, COVID crash, and recovery periods.

3

Configure the Strategy

The Moving Average Crossover strategy buys when the fast MA crosses above the slow MA, and sells on the opposite cross. Adjust the Fast MA and Slow MA windows to test different sensitivities.

4

Real NEPSE Costs Included

Unlike basic simulators, NEPSE QuantFlow models actual Nepal market costs: 0.4% broker commission, 0.015% SEBON fee, and NPR 25 DP charge per transaction. These small costs significantly impact profitability.

5

Analyze Results

After running, you'll see: Total Return, Sharpe Ratio (risk-adjusted returns), Max Drawdown (worst loss), Win Rate, a full equity curve chart, and a complete trade-by-trade log.
