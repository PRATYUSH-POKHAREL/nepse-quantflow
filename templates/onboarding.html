Welcome to NEPSE QuantFlow

Nepal's first professional backtesting platform for the stock market.

What is this platform?

NEPSE QuantFlow lets you test trading strategies on historical Nepali stock data without risking a single rupee. It's like a flight simulator for stock trading — you practice with past data before trading with real money.

What is NEPSE?

NEPSE (Nepal Stock Exchange) is Nepal's only stock exchange, based in Kathmandu. Over 200 companies are listed here — banks, hydropower, insurance, manufacturing, and more. Trading happens Sunday to Thursday, 11 AM to 3 PM.

What is Backtesting?

Backtesting is applying your trading rules to old market data to see if your strategy would have made money or lost money. If a strategy fails on past data, it will almost certainly fail in the future. Backtesting saves you from costly mistakes.

How Stock Trading Works

The absolute basics every trader must know.

📈

Buying (Long Position)

You buy shares hoping the price will go up. If you buy at NPR 500 and sell at NPR 600, you profit NPR 100 per share.

📉

Selling / Exiting

When you sell your shares to exit a position. In Nepal, delivery-based trading means you must own shares to sell them.

💸

Broker Commission

Every time you buy or sell, your broker charges ~0.4% of the trade value. This adds up fast.

🏛️

SEBON & DP Fees

SEBON (regulator) takes 0.015%. DP (Demat) charges NPR 25 per transaction. These small costs kill small profits.

⚠️ Key Lesson: In Nepal, buying and selling one stock can cost ~1% in fees. Your strategy must make more than 1% just to break even.

Moving Average Crossover Strategy

The strategy you'll be testing on this platform.

What is a Moving Average (MA)?

A moving average is simply the average closing price over a certain number of days. It smooths out daily price noise and shows the underlying trend.

  • 20-day MA = Average of last 20 days' closing prices (shows short-term trend)
  • 50-day MA = Average of last 50 days' closing prices (shows medium-term trend)

How the Crossover Strategy Works

There are two moving averages — a fast one and a slow one. When they cross, it signals a trend change:

🟢

Golden Cross → BUY

When the fast MA crosses ABOVE the slow MA. This signals that prices are trending upward. The strategy buys shares.

🔴

Death Cross → SELL

When the fast MA crosses BELOW the slow MA. This signals that prices are trending downward. The strategy sells shares.

💡 Pro Tip: MA crossover is a trend-following strategy. It works best in strongly trending markets and poorly in sideways/choppy markets. There is no perfect strategy — backtesting helps you understand when it works and when it fails.

Understanding the Results

What each number on the dashboard actually means.

Total Return

The percentage your capital grew (or shrank). If you started with NPR 1,00,000 and ended with NPR 1,15,000, your total return is +15%. Negative means you lost money.

Sharpe Ratio

Measures return per unit of risk. Think of it as "how much reward did I get for the rollercoaster ride?"

  • Above 1.0 = Good (decent returns for the risk taken)
  • Above 2.0 = Excellent
  • Below 0.5 = Not worth the stress
  • Negative = You lost money while taking risk. Worst case.

Max Drawdown

The biggest drop from a peak to a trough. If your portfolio went from NPR 1,50,000 down to NPR 1,00,000, that's a -33% drawdown. It measures the maximum pain you'd have endured. Can you handle watching your money drop that much?

Win Rate

Percentage of trades that were profitable. A 40% win rate can still be profitable if winners are bigger than losers. Don't judge a strategy by win rate alone.

How to Use This Platform

Step-by-step walkthrough of the dashboard.

1. Pick a Stock

Choose from 50+ NEPSE stocks across banking, hydropower, insurance, microfinance, and other sectors. Each stock behaves differently.

2. Set Date Range

Select any period from 2020 to 2026. Longer periods give more reliable results. Include both bull and bear markets to see how your strategy handles different conditions.

3. Adjust MA Windows

Change the Fast MA (default 20) and Slow MA (default 50). Try different combinations:

  • 5/20 — Very sensitive, more trades, higher costs
  • 20/50 — Balanced, moderate trades
  • 50/200 — Slow, fewer trades, long-term trend

4. Run & Analyze

Click Run Backtest. You'll see your equity curve (how your money grew), all metrics, and every buy/sell trade with exact dates and prices.

⚠️ Critical Warnings

  • Past performance ≠ future results. A strategy that worked from 2020-2024 may fail tomorrow.
  • Transaction costs matter. NEPSE fees are high. A "profitable" strategy before costs may lose money after costs.
  • This is a learning tool, not financial advice. Never trade real money based solely on a backtest.
  • Start small. If you ever trade real money, begin with amounts you can afford to lose completely.
