🔮 Walk-Forward Optimization

Test if your strategy is truly robust or overfitted to past data. Uses rolling training/testing windows.

How Walk-Forward Works

The data is split into rolling windows. Each window has a training period (in-sample) and a testing period (out-of-sample). If a strategy performs well in out-of-sample windows, it's more likely to be robust and not overfit.
